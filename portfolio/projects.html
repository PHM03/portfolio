Campus Room Booking | IoT

e-RoomReserve: AI-Driven Smart Campus Space Optimization System with Real-Time Occupancy Detection and Autonomous Reservation Control using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Technology

St. Dominic College of Asia manages multiple classrooms and meeting spaces that require efficient scheduling and monitoring to maximize utilization. Traditional reservation systems rely on manual booking and periodic physical checks, which lead to no-shows, double bookings, unverified use of spaces, and inefficient space allocation. Advances in Internet of Things (IoT) technologies — specifically Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacon technology — enable low-cost, real-time verification of room occupancy. This project proposes a campus-tailored solution that integrates a mobile and web reservation application, a faculty approval workflow, BLE-based presence detection and occupancy monitoring, an automated staff notification system, and an AI-based room recommendation feature that assists users in selecting appropriate spaces based on availability and room attributes, while incorporating post-reservation feedback analysis to support facility management decisions.

Online Ordering | Community Commerce

SariSite: Enhancing Accessibility of Localized Sari-Sari Stores in Philippine Subdivision Through Online Ordering

Sari-sari stores are an integral part of Filipino communities, serving as convenient sources of daily necessities within neighborhoods. However, many of these stores remain limited to traditional face-to-face transactions, which can be time-consuming and less accessible to residents with busy schedules or mobility constraints. With the increasing reliance on digital platforms for everyday needs, integrating an online ordering system for localized sari-sari stores offers a practical solution to improve accessibility and convenience. This study explores how such a system can enhance customer experience, streamline store operations, and strengthen the role of sari-sari stores in Philippine subdivisions.
